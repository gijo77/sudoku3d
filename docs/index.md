# Privacy Policy — Sudoku 3D

_Last updated: 2026-11-21_

This policy describes how the **Sudoku 3D** app (package `com.aitechplay.sudoku3d`, "the app") handles
information. It is provided by **Gijo** ("we", "us"). Contact: **eshaji90@gmail.com**.

## Summary
**We do not run any servers and we do not collect, share or sell personal data.**
The game works fully offline. If you choose to use **Google Play Games**, Google stores your achievements,
leaderboard scores and a backup of your game progress in your Google Play Games account.

## Information stored on your device
The app saves the following in its private app storage on your device:
- your settings (sound, music, vibration, theme, highlight options, graphics quality);
- your game statistics (games played, wins, best times, completed daily puzzles);
- your rewards progress (XP, rank and medals earned);
- your game in progress, so you can resume it.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings › Apps › Sudoku 3D › Storage,
or by uninstalling the app.

## Google Play Games (optional)
The app uses **Google Play Games Services**, provided by Google, for:
- **Sign-in** with your Play Games profile (Google may sign you in automatically if you use Play Games);
- **Achievements**, which mirror the medals you earn in the game;
- **Leaderboards** for best solve time per difficulty and total XP;
- **Saved Games (cloud backup)** of your statistics and rewards progress, so you can restore them on a new device.

When you are signed in, the app sends this game data to Google: your achievement unlocks, your leaderboard
scores, and a backup file containing your statistics and rewards progress (no contacts, location or other
personal content). Google links this data to your Play Games profile. Leaderboard scores may be visible to other
players depending on your Play Games profile settings. We, the developer, can see aggregate achievement and
leaderboard information in Google Play Console; we do not receive your email address.

This data is processed by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the
Google Play Games terms. You can choose not to sign in, sign out, or manage and delete your Play Games data in
the Google Play Games settings on your device or in your Google Account. The game keeps working without it.

## Permissions
- **INTERNET**: used only by Google Play Games Services (sign-in, achievements, leaderboards, cloud backup).
- **VIBRATE**: optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

The app requests no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

## Third parties
The only third-party service is Google Play Games Services, described above. The app contains no advertising,
analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs and no in-app purchases.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Google Play Games sign-in is optional
and follows Google's own rules for child accounts.

## Changes to this policy
If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and the Play Store listing before releasing that version.
