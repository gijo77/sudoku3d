# Privacy Policy — Sudoku 3D

_Last updated: 2026-11-18_

This policy describes how the **Sudoku 3D** app (package `com.aitechplay.sudoku3d`, "the app") handles
information. It is provided by **Gijo** ("we", "us"). Contact: **eshaji90@gmail.com**.

## Summary
**The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data.**

## Information we collect
None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no
in-app purchases. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data off your device.

## Information stored on your device
To work offline, the app saves the following **only on your device**, in its private app storage:
- your settings (sound, music, vibration, theme, highlight options, graphics quality);
- your game statistics (games played, wins, best times, completed daily puzzles);
- your rewards progress (XP, rank and medals earned);
- your game in progress, so you can resume it.

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in
Android Settings › Apps › Sudoku 3D › Storage, or by uninstalling the app.

## Permissions
The app declares only the standard **VIBRATE** permission (for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in
Settings). It requests no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage or network.

## Third parties
The app contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or tracking.

## Children
The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy
If the app's data practices ever change (for example, if ads or online features are added), we will update this
policy and the Play Store listing before releasing that version.
