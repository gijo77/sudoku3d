## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Sudoku 3D turns the classic number puzzle into a glowing 3D board you can tilt, zoom and play anywhere. It works fully offline, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

**CLASSIC SUDOKU, IN 3D**
• Raised 3D tiles with crisp numbers, a neon-lit board and smooth animations
• Drag to tilt the board, pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset the view
• Four difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert
• Every puzzle is generated fresh and has exactly one solution, so no guessing is needed

**BONUS: 3D CUBE SUDOKU**
A new twist: a 4x4x4 cube of four stacked layers. Each layer is a 4x4 Sudoku, and every vertical pillar must contain 1 to 4 exactly once. Rotate the cube to see every layer.

**PLAY YOUR WAY**
• Pencil notes, undo / redo, erase and smart hints
• Number pad that shows how many of each digit are left
• Highlights for the row, column and box, and for matching numbers (you choose)
• Three lives, a timer and a satisfying wave when you finish a row, column or box
• Daily puzzle: the same puzzle for everyone each day, with a streak counter

**RANKS, MEDALS AND REWARDS**
• Earn XP for every solve, with bonuses for harder levels, no-mistake games and the daily puzzle
• Rank up from Tile Newbie to Sudoku Sage
• Collect six medals in Bronze, Silver and Gold: Winner, Flawless, Warm Streak, Cube Explorer, Speedster and Expert Mind
• Unlock new colour themes as you rank up: Ocean, Forest and Sunset

**GOOGLE PLAY GAMES (OPTIONAL)**
• 18 achievements that match your medals
• Leaderboards for the fastest solve on every difficulty, plus total XP
• Cloud backup of your stats and rewards, so your progress follows you to a new phone
• Prefer to stay offline? Skip sign-in and everything still works

**TRACK YOUR PROGRESS**
• Games played, win rate and best time for every difficulty, in Classic and Cube
• A 28-day calendar of your daily puzzles
• Your game is saved automatically, so you can pick up exactly where you left off

**MADE TO FEEL GOOD**
• A futuristic neon look with glass panels and a glowing 3D board
• Five colour themes: Light, Dark, Ocean, Forest and Sunset
• Relaxing music and soft sound effects (easy to switch off), optional vibration
• Runs smoothly on phones of all sizes; a high-quality graphics switch keeps older devices fast

**PRIVACY FIRST**
No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. The internet is used only if you sign in to Google Play Games, for achievements, leaderboards and cloud backup.

Download Sudoku 3D and give your brain a calm, beautiful workout.
