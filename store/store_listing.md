## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Sudoku 3D turns the classic number puzzle into a tactile 3D board you can tilt, zoom and play anywhere —
completely offline, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

**CLASSIC SUDOKU, IN 3D**
• Raised 3D tiles with embossed numbers, soft shadows and smooth animations
• Drag to tilt the board, pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset the view
• Four difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert
• Every puzzle is generated fresh and has exactly one solution — no guessing needed

**BONUS: 3D CUBE SUDOKU**
Try a new twist: a 4x4x4 cube of four stacked layers. Each layer is a 4x4 Sudoku, and every vertical
pillar must contain 1–4 exactly once. Rotate the cube to see every layer.

**PLAY YOUR WAY**
• Pencil notes, undo / redo, erase and smart hints
• Number pad that shows how many of each digit are left
• Highlights for the row, column and box — and for matching numbers (you choose)
• Mistake counter (3 strikes), timer and a satisfying wave when you finish a row, column or box
• Daily puzzle: the same puzzle for everyone each day, works offline, with a streak counter

**TRACK YOUR PROGRESS**
• Games played, win rate and best time for every difficulty
• Your game is saved automatically — pick up exactly where you left off

**MADE TO FEEL GOOD**
• Five colour themes: Light, Dark, Ocean, Forest and Sunset
• Relaxing music and soft sound effects (easy to switch off), optional vibration
• Runs smoothly on phones of all sizes; a high-quality graphics switch keeps older devices fast

**PRIVACY FIRST**
Sudoku 3D needs no internet permission. It collects no personal data, shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.

Download Sudoku 3D and give your brain a calm, beautiful workout.
